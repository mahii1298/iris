About IRIS

A Benchmark in Quality, Reliability & Safety

Introducing Integrity Reliability Inspection Service (IRIS) is an adaptable, ISO 9001:2015 certified engineering service provider in the specialized field of Non-Destructive Testing and Quality Assurance.

Headquartered with sophisticated laboratory facilities at Thiruverumbur, Trichy—adjacent to premier industrial clusters—IRIS addresses challenging demands in engineering inspection. Our primary mission is to maximize your plant's uptime by minimizing downtime through fast, consistent, and creative non-destructive inspection techniques.

For Industries

Comprehensive flaw detection in welds, boilers, castings, and pipelines ensuring zero deleterious effect on stressed assets.

For Students & Engineers

Practical-first training with standard calibration blocks and real industrial defect samples under senior Level III guidance.
